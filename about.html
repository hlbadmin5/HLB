About Harmony's Liquor Box

Serving Clay County with a curated selection and hometown service.

Our Story

Conveniently located along historic US-40 in Harmony, Indiana, Harmony's Liquor Box has been proudly serving the local community and travelers alike for over two and a half years. What started as a goal to provide a reliable, well-stocked local bottle shop has grown into Clay County's premier destination for everyday staples and hard-to-find spirits.

We pride ourselves on offering a clean, friendly, and welcoming atmosphere where you can find cold craft beer, fine wines, premium spirits, and personalized recommendations.

Store Leadership

Store Manager: Beth Morse

Under the management of Beth Morse, our team is dedicated to customer service, knowledgeable guidance, and maintaining a diverse inventory tailored to what our customers want to drink.

Our Commitment

Whether you are picking up a quick weekend 6-pack, looking for the perfect bottle of wine for dinner, or hunting for allocated bourbon releases, we work directly with distributors to bring top-shelf quality right here to Harmony.
